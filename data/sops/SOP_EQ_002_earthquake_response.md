# SOP-EQ-002: Earthquake & Structural Collapse Response Protocol

> **ACADEMIC DISCLAIMER**: This is a synthetic operational document generated solely for academic evaluation in CSE476 (Agentic AI and Intelligent Automation). Do not use for actual civil defense or disaster deployment.

## 1. Purpose and Operational Scope
Governs emergency response following high-magnitude seismic events ($\ge 6.0$ Richter scale) accompanied by structural collapse, widespread orthopedic trauma, and severed lifeline utilities.

## 2. Damage Assessment and Structural Severity
- **Level 1 (Slight Damage):** Superficial plaster cracks; structural frames intact; zero utility disruptions.
- **Level 2 (Moderate Damage):** Partition wall fractures; partial chimney collapses; localized power/water cuts.
- **Level 3 (Heavy Damage):** Severe structural wall buckling; 15%–30% partial roof collapses; hospital facilities degraded.
- **Level 4 (Severe Structural Failure):** Over 50% multi-story buildings collapsed; urban search & rescue (USAR) active.
- **Level 5 (Catastrophic Flattening):** Near-total structural collapse across entire municipal footprint; mass trauma.

## 3. Humanitarian Resource Allocation Ratios
Seismic emergencies require intense medical and shelter prioritization over pure volumetric food:
- **Trauma Medical Kits:** 1 Level-A Major Surgical & Orthopedic Trauma Kit per 100 affected persons at medical urgency $\ge 0.70$. Contains tourniquets, bone splints, sterile dressings, and plasma expanders.
- **Emergency Family Tents:** 1 weatherproof family shelter tent per 5 persons displaced. In seismic zones, 70% to 100% of residents refuse to re-enter standing masonry due to aftershock panic.
- **Potable Drinking Water:** 10.0 liters per person per day (minimum survival standard while municipal aqueducts are fractured).
- **Ready-to-Eat Food Rations:** High-calorie, non-cook survival rations (2,100 kcal/person/day) requiring zero gas or electricity to prepare.

## 4. Priority Weight Multipliers for Earthquakes
Seismic response formulation elevates medical urgency:
- **Medical Urgency Weight ($w_m$):** 0.40 (primary factor during initial 72-hour Golden Window)
- **Structural Damage Severity Weight ($w_d$):** 0.30
- **Demographic Vulnerability Weight ($w_v$):** 0.20
- **Population Density Weight ($w_p$):** 0.10

## 5. Secondary Hazard and Aftershock Rules
- All staging warehouses within 50 km of the epicenter must undergo rapid structural audit before forklift dispatch.
- When an aftershock of magnitude $\ge 5.0$ occurs mid-run, the Relief Coordinator must issue an immediate `BLACKBOARD_INVALIDATE` event to trigger re-planning.
