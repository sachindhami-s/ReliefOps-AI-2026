# SOP-LS-003: Landslide & Debris Flow Emergency Response Protocol

> **ACADEMIC DISCLAIMER**: This is a synthetic operational document generated solely for academic evaluation in CSE476 (Agentic AI and Intelligent Automation). Do not use for actual civil defense or disaster deployment.

## 1. Purpose and Operational Scope
Directs emergency operations in mountainous or hilly terrain where slope failures, mass mudflow, and rockfalls isolate high-elevation settlements and sever single-artery mountain transit highways.

## 2. Terrain and Road Impassability Scoring
- **Level 1 (Rockfall Hazard):** Intermittent debris on shoulder; single-lane alternating transit maintained.
- **Level 2 (Partial Slide):** Road blocked along 50m sector; light vehicles cleared within 6 hours.
- **Level 3 (Major Slope Failure):** Road blocked along 200m; heavy machinery clearance requires 24–48 hours; valley isolated.
- **Level 4 (Multi-Sector Blockade):** Multiple bridges crushed; entire tributary valley severed from terrestrial supply.
- **Level 5 (Village Burial / Mountain Collapse):** Settlement completely buried under debris; ground approach impossible.

## 3. Resource Requirements in Isolated Zones
When mountain valleys are cut off:
- **Emergency Aerial Airdrop Water:** 5.0 to 10.0 liters per person per day packaged in high-impact collapsible containers.
- **Compact Survival Nutrition Packs:** High-energy nutrient paste / dehydrated ration bars (2,400 kcal/person/day in cold altitudes).
- **Hypothermia & Mountain Medical Packs:** Specialized trauma kits with burn dressings, crush injury stabilizers, and cold-exposure treatments.
- **High-Altitude Emergency Tents:** Alpine-rated thermal shelter units with insulated ground tarps.

## 4. Logistics and Transit Impedance Rules
- Terrestrial winding factor in mountain terrain increases from baseline $1.35$ to $W_t = 1.85$.
- Average transit velocity over mountain roads under landslide alert drops to $V_{base} = 25 \text{ km/h}$.
- When road access status is `BLOCKED`, terrestrial dispatch is canceled and flagged for forward staging at the nearest accessible airfield or helipad.
