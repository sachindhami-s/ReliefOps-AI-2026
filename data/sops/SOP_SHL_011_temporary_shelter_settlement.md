# SOP-SHL-011: Temporary Shelter & Family Settlement Protocol

## 1. Purpose and Operational Scope
Governs the assessment, allocation, and delivery of emergency family shelter tents and winterized relief tarpaulins for displaced populations whose dwellings have been damaged or rendered uninhabitable.

## 2. Quantitative Shelter Standards (Sphere Shelter Standards)
- **Covered Living Space:** Minimum **3.5 square meters per person** of covered living space in emergency settlements.
- **Family Shelter Unit (SKU: `SHELTER_TENTS`):**
  - Weatherproof, flame-retardant multi-layered canvas tent ($16.0 \text{ m}^2$) engineered for a household of 5 individuals ($3.2 \text{ m}^2 / \text{person}$).
  - Packed kit includes: Aluminum modular ridge frame, heavy ground tarp, stakes, guy lines, and mallet.
- **Displacement Calculation Norm:**
  $$\text{DisplacedHouseholds}_i = \left\lceil \frac{\text{Population}_i \times (0.10 + 0.18 \cdot \text{DamageLevel}_i)}{5} \right\rceil$$

## 3. Prioritization and Climate Modifiers
- In flood environments, prioritize elevated non-inundated embankments or designated staging schools for tent deployment.
- High wind areas require double-pegged reinforced guy lines.
- Zones with $> 40\%$ damaged residential structures receive immediate top-priority tent dispatch.
