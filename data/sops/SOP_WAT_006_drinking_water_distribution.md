# SOP-WAT-006: Potable Drinking Water Distribution & Minimum Norms

> **ACADEMIC DISCLAIMER**: This is a synthetic operational document generated solely for academic evaluation in CSE476 (Agentic AI and Intelligent Automation). Do not use for actual civil defense or disaster deployment.

## 1. Purpose and Operational Scope
Defines mandatory quantitative standards for clean drinking water supply, filtration units, and container distribution to prevent dehydration and fatal waterborne disease outbreaks in disaster aftermaths.

## 2. Quantitative Water Supply Standards (Sphere Standards)
Water requirements vary by operational response phase:
- **Phase 1: Immediate Survival Threshold (Days 1–3):**
  - Minimum **7.5 to 10.0 liters per person per day** (3.0L strictly for drinking and survival hydration; 4.5L for basic food preparation).
- **Phase 2: Stabilized Relief Standard (Days 4–30):**
  - Minimum **15.0 liters per person per day** (covers drinking, cooking, and minimal personal hygiene to suppress cholera/hepatitis-A).
- **Special Operational Allowance:**
  - Hospitals, field clinics, and cholera treatment centers require **50.0 liters per in-patient per day**.

## 3. Water Packaging and Delivery Units
- **10-Liter Rigid Jerrycans:** Standard unit SKU `CLEAN_WATER`. Stackable, food-grade polyethylene containers with tamper-evident caps.
- **Chlorine Water Purification Tablets (NaDCC):** 1 strip of 10 tablets (purifies 100 liters) dispatched alongside every 2 jerrycans in contaminated water zones.

## 4. Priority Allocation and Quality Assurance
- When total warehouse water supplies cover $< 50\%$ of aggregate regional need, the allocation engine must enforce a **Strict Survival Cap**: limit initial dispatch to 7.5 L/person-day across all zones before authorizing secondary 15.0 L/person-day allowances.
- **Fairness Constraint:** No accessible zone may receive $< 3.0 \text{ L/person-day}$ under any circumstance.
