# SOP-CY-004: Cyclone & Coastal Storm Surge Emergency Response Protocol

> **ACADEMIC DISCLAIMER**: This is a synthetic operational document generated solely for academic evaluation in CSE476 (Agentic AI and Intelligent Automation). Do not use for actual civil defense or disaster deployment.

## 1. Purpose and Operational Scope
Provides binding guidelines for disaster response when tropical cyclones make landfall, creating simultaneous extreme wind destruction (Category 3–5, $> 120 \text{ km/h}$), coastal storm surges ($2.0\text{m}–5.0\text{m}$), and extensive saline intrusion into groundwater.

## 2. Cyclone Destruction Severity Metrics
- **Level 1 (Gale Force Warning):** Winds 60–90 km/h; thatched roofs damaged; coastal fishing suspended.
- **Level 2 (Severe Cyclone):** Winds 90–120 km/h; power distribution poles uprooted; trees block secondary roads.
- **Level 3 (Very Severe Cyclone):** Winds 120–160 km/h; storm surge 1.5–2.5m; coastal embankments breached; telecommunications down.
- **Level 4 (Extremely Severe Cyclone):** Winds 160–220 km/h; surge > 3.0m; brick masonry structures de-roofed; widespread saline flooding.
- **Level 5 (Super Cyclone):** Winds > 220 km/h; catastrophic total destruction of coastal belt; universal evacuation.

## 3. Immediate Humanitarian Ration Requirements
- **Desalination / Bottled Water Supply:** Because coastal storm surge floods coastal groundwater with seawater, local wells are 100% undrinkable. Supply **minimum 15.0 liters of potable water per person per day** exclusively from inland warehouses.
- **Dry Sealed Food Rations:** Waterproof packaging is mandatory. Food must be protected against driving rain and surge dampness.
- **Emergency Tarpaulins & Tie-Down Tents:** Heavy-duty reinforced PVC tarpaulins (4m x 6m) with nylon tie-down ropes (minimum 2 tarps per family of 5).
- **Anti-Venom & Waterborne Medical Kits:** Due to displaced snake populations in surge waters, medical kits must include polyvalent anti-venom and water-purification reagents.

## 4. Priority Multipliers for Coastal Zones
- Coastal zones within 10 km of coastline with storm surge elevation $> 2.0m$ receive an automatic **+25 point boost** to their calculated Zone Priority Score ($ZPS$).
- Inland staging warehouses situated outside the storm surge inundation zone must be selected as primary supply origins.
