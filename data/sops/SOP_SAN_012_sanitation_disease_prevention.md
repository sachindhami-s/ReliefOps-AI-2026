# SOP-SAN-012: Sanitation, Hygiene & Epidemic Control Protocol

> **ACADEMIC DISCLAIMER**: This is a synthetic operational document generated solely for academic evaluation in CSE476 (Agentic AI and Intelligent Automation). Do not use for actual civil defense or disaster deployment.

## 1. Purpose and Operational Scope
Provides binding guidelines for implementing sanitation facilities, distributing emergency hygiene kits, disinfecting flood water zones, and preventing post-disaster enteric disease outbreaks (cholera, typhoid, dysentery).

## 2. Quantitative Sanitation Standards
- **Excreta Disposal:** Maximum **20 persons per shared emergency latrine** with separate, lockable cubicles for women and vulnerable groups.
- **Handwashing Facilities:** Minimum 1 functional handwashing station with soap per 50 persons.
- **Hygiene Kits:** 1 family hygiene pack per 5 persons every 30 days containing biodegradable antibacterial soap, toothpaste, feminine sanitary towels, washing powder, and plastic bucket with tap.

## 3. Disinfection and Vector Control
- In waterlogged post-flood conditions, stagnant pools within 500 meters of human settlements must be treated with larvicide within 72 hours of water stabilization.
- Disinfection teams must be deployed concurrently with water ration distribution in all Level 4 and Level 5 flood sectors.
